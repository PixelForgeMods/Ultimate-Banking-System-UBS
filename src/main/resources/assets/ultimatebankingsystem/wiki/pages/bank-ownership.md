# Owning a bank

> Charters, tellers, premises and owner controls.

## Starting out

Use `/bank help` to inspect player bank commands. Bank creation may require a charter, funds, playtime or operator approval depending on server settings.

A custom bank's new customer accounts are opened through its teller, not `/account open`.

## Operations

The Bank Owner PC manages premises, staff, bank policies, accounts, safety features and reporting. Set up a valid premise and staff so customers can use the bank.

Bank limits and interest rates can be constrained by server policy. A bank may be suspended, locked down or revoked; its status affects access.
