# Using an ATM

> Sign in to check balances, move money and manage requests.

## Sign in

Right-click an ATM and select an account. Your primary account is selected when available. On first use, create and confirm a four-digit PIN; later visits ask for that PIN. Keep it private.

![ATM PIN entry](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:06?width=853&height=610)

## Choose an action

The ATM menu offers balance inquiry, cash withdrawal and deposit, digital transfers, history, account settings and payment requests.

![ATM main menu](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:07?width=851&height=602)

Withdrawals dispense whole-dollar bills. Enter the amount and confirm; account balance, transaction limits and daily limits all apply. A denial does not dispense cash.

![ATM cash withdrawal](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:08?width=847&height=607)

For a deposit, carry supported UBS bills or coins. The ATM counts available tender, so the requested amount must be representable by the items you carry. Bundled stacks may need unwrapping first.

![ATM cash deposit](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:09?width=851&height=610)

Transfers move digital funds between accounts without producing physical cash. Check the recipient and amount before confirming.

![ATM transfer confirmation](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:10?width=845&height=605)

Payment requests appear in their own ATM view; review the requester before paying or declining. Transaction History shows the result of completed operations.

![ATM payment requests](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:11?width=847&height=611)

![ATM transaction history](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:03?width=850&height=610)

If an action fails, check the PIN, account status, limits, inventory tender and available balance.
