# Selling job products

> Find eligible goods, inspect the live quote and confirm a sale.

## Visit the buyer

Talk to the buyer NPC for your active role. The catalog lists eligible products, category filters, prices and how many matching items you carry. Search by item name to narrow the list.

![Lumberjack Buyer NPC](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:57?width=2560&height=1440)

![Buyer catalog with prices and carried count](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:58?width=2088&height=1247)

Select a product and set the quantity. **Preview Sale** shows the final amount; **Confirm Sale** removes matching goods and pays the account. If a review is required, confirm that step too before expecting the payout.

![Review-required sale with final confirmation](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:59?width=2097&height=1250)

Only matching unmodified goods are taken for ordinary job sales. Named, enchanted or otherwise component-modified stacks are not silently converted.

## Why quotes vary

Category, job level, supply limits and daily spotlights can affect the price. The buyer's live quote is authoritative; similarly named items may have different values. Daily quests rotate on a real-world UTC day, while earnings and supply limits may follow the server's Minecraft-day settings.
