# Transfers and requests

> Move money safely between players and accounts.

## Send and transfer

`/account send <player> <amount>` sends from your primary account. `/account transfer <fromAccountId> <toAccountId> <amount>` moves money between accounts.

You can also use the ATM transfer screen. Review the destination before confirming; transfers are real account transactions.

## Payment requests

`/account payrequest <player> <amount> [destinationAccountId]` asks another player to pay. The recipient can review, accept or decline from chat or the ATM Pay Requests screen.

A request is not a payment until the recipient accepts it.
