## Using physical cash

Right-click a block with a bill or coin to place a cash pile. Use the *same kind* on that pile to add to it, up to eight pieces per block. Break the pile to recover its items.

![Placed cash piles in the world](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:22?width=1166&height=1067)

Dollar bills can also become [100-bill money stacks](wiki:money-stacks). A separate bank note, cheque, gold bar or silver bar is **not** one of the cash denominations above.
