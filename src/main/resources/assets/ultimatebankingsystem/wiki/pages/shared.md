# Joint and business accounts

> Share account access with clear ownership.

## Joint accounts

`/account joint create <player> <bankName>` creates a shared account. `/account joint info <accountId>` shows its members and state.

Joint deposit, withdraw, transfer and close commands are listed by `/account help`. Check each bank's eligibility and limits before creating one.

## Business accounts

`/account business create <label> <bankName>` opens a business account where allowed. The owner can grant or revoke roles and transfer ownership with the `/account business` commands.

Assign only the access needed for a player's duties; business account ownership and individual access are distinct.
