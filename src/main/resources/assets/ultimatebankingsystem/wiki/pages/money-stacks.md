# Money straps and stacks

> Turn 100 matching bills into one compact, placeable money-stack item.

## Bundle bills

Craft a denomination-specific **strap** from paper and dye. Put one strap and at least **100 bills of the same denomination** in a crafting grid. The special bundling recipe consumes one strap and exactly 100 bills per result; extra bills remain. Straps of a different denomination do not work.

The examples show the dye arrangement for each denomination, from $1 through $100.

## Place or unwrap

![Combining a strap with matching bills](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:21?width=366&height=202)

Right-click a block with a money-stack item to place one bundle. Right-click an existing pile with a matching bundle to add another, up to **eight bundles**. Different denominations cannot share one pile. Breaking the pile drops its bundle items.

**Sneak-right-click** while holding a bundle to unwrap it into 100 loose bills. The strap is **consumed**, not returned. If your inventory is full, surplus bills drop nearby.

Some cash purchases count carried bundles as 100 bills and automatically break a needed bundle, returning any unused loose bills. An ATM deposit checks its own supported cash input; unwrap bundles first when it asks for loose tender.
