# Shopping in stores

> Shop at a shelf or order deliveries through the Retail Webshop.

## In-world checkout

Inspect a display's item, price and stock. Add products to a basket, then use the linked cashier to review the basket and pay. Opening hours and stock may prevent a purchase even when your account has funds.

![Shopping basket beside in-world shelves](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:31?width=2560&height=1440)

![Cashier checkout screen](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:32?width=2560&height=1440)

Read the final total and payment method before confirming. A failed payment does not complete the sale. For fixed-terminal behavior, see [Payment terminals](wiki:terminals).

## Retail Webshop

Open Retail Webshop from the desktop's Utilities area. The Home view shows the current shop context. Browse Catalog, inspect prices and available quantities, then add items to Cart.

![Retail Webshop home](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:33?width=2560&height=1440)

![Retail Webshop catalog](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:34?width=2560&height=1440)

![Reviewing the webshop cart](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:35?width=2560&height=1440)

At Checkout, verify quantities and total. Select the account or other offered payment method before placing the order; the checkout choices depend on the shop and your accounts.

![Webshop checkout](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:36?width=2560&height=1440)

![Webshop payment selection](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:37?width=2560&height=1440)

Choose a delivery location and mode. A specific-pallet delivery needs an eligible shop and pallet target. The location and mode shown during checkout determine where the order goes; they are not changed by viewing Tracking later.

![Selecting the delivery location](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:38?width=2560&height=1440)

![Selecting the delivery mode](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:39?width=2560&height=1440)

![Choosing a specific delivery pallet](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:40?width=2560&height=1440)

## Track an order

Tracking distinguishes queued, delivered and failed orders. Select an order to read its status and delivery details. A queued order may still offer **Cancel and refund** before delivery processing begins; once processing starts, rely on the displayed status and available actions.

![Queued order and recovery details in Tracking](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:41?width=2560&height=1440)
