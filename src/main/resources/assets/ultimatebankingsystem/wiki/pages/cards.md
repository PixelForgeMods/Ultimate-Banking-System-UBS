# Cards and phone

> Bank cards, mobile access and contactless payment.

## Cards and terminals

Use a card linked to an account at supported payment terminals, or pay from the eligible primary account when the terminal offers that route. A failed or denied payment does not settle the purchase.

Bank tellers and account settings handle card-related access; keep cards and PINs private.

## Smartphone

Right-click the UBS smartphone or use its configured keybind to open it. Phone apps include account views, messages, notes, contacts and supported payments.

![UBS smartphone](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:26?width=523&height=1123)

Phone and bank-app authentication are separate safeguards. If an app is locked, follow its on-screen PIN or password prompt.
