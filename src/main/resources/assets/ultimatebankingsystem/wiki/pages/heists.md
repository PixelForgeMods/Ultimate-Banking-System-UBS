# Bank heists

> Plan a crew, breach a bank and extract.

## Plan

Open the planner with `/heist`, `/heist plan` or `/bank heist`. Choose an eligible bank, invite crew, ready up and start the countdown.

A target can be unavailable because of cooldowns, premise setup, protection or another active heist. The planner reports eligibility.

## Act and leave

Heist tools include masks, lockpicks, saws, drills and RFID spoofers. Follow the HUD and in-world prompts to breach, collect supported loot and reach extraction before time runs out.

`/heist abandon` leaves the heist. If the last crew member leaves, the heist ends. Moving too far from the target can also abandon participation.
