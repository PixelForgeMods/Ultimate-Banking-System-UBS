# Balance HUD and pickpocket

> Personal display and cash-risk settings.

## Balance HUD

`/account hud toggle` shows or hides the balance display. `/account hud primary` tracks the primary account; `/account hud account <accountId>` follows a specific one.

Move it with `/account hud move <position>`. Command suggestions provide supported positions. The HUD is read-only; account actions still happen at the bank, ATM or account menu.

## Pickpocket

`/account pickpocket status` checks your opt-in state. `/account pickpocket toggle` changes it. When disabled, you cannot pickpocket others and are protected from player pickpocket attempts.

The pickpocket keybind is rebindable in Minecraft controls.
