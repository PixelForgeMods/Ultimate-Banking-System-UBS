# The Job Market

> Choose a role and earn digital UBS money through its work.

## Apply

Talk to a Job Market NPC or run `/ubs job`. Select an open role and choose Apply for Job. Acceptance is immediate; the status changes to On Shift. `/ubs job status` reopens the market view.

## Work and payout

Most roles sell eligible goods to their role's buyer NPC. Courier uses deliveries instead. A **primary UBS account** is needed for digital payouts. Each role keeps separate XP, levels, daily quests and achievements.

The roles are Lumberjack, Miner, Fisherman, Farmer, Hunter, Blacksmith, Courier, Chef, Herbalist, Salvager and Tailor. The corresponding NPC must be available in the world for its work flow.

Use `/ubs job leave` to leave your current role. Earned progression is kept, but outstanding work closes. See [Selling job products](wiki:job-selling), [Job progress](wiki:job-progress) and [Courier deliveries](wiki:courier).
