# Safes, RFID and alarms

> Secure property and configure access.

## RFID

RFID scanners and cards control access to linked outputs. Owners configure scanner identity, authorization and success or failure behavior; a card must be authorized for the reader and access level.

## Safe activity

Safe and vault management records access and activity for supported doors and inventory surfaces. Review the activity panel to investigate openings, closings or removed contents.

## Alarms

Bank alarm settings live in the Safe app. A custom .ogg can be imported from the client file picker. Test Sound restarts the preview; Stop Test stops it.

On a dedicated server, each player needs the sound available to their client to hear a custom local import.
