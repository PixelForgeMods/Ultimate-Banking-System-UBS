# Levels, quests and achievements

> Track a role's XP, rotating quests and claimable rewards.

## Career progress

The Progress screen shows current level, XP toward the next level and a roadmap. Select a level node to inspect its unlocks. Every fifth level is a milestone; later levels can improve payout, XP or daily capacity. Locked benefits do not apply early.

## Daily quests

Quest cards show a concrete target, current progress and time until reset. They rotate on a real-world UTC day, not after `/time add`. A completed quest is marked Complete; check the card before the reset.

![Three completed daily quest cards](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:62?width=2016&height=287)

## Achievements

The Achievements view tracks harvesting, selling, quest completion, rank and other role-specific goals. A finished objective may show **Claim Reward**; claim it once to receive its digital money, job XP or item reward. In Progress does not mean the reward has been paid.
