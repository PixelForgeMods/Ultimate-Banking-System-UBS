# Loans and credit

> Borrowing, repayments and credit status.

## Borrowing

`/account loan request <amount>` previews a personal loan. This is a quote, not an immediate payout. Review the amount, interest and repayment terms before using `/account loan confirm` to accept the pending offer.

![Loan offer preview in chat](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:29?width=997&height=347)

`/account loan status` lists active loans and their current repayment state.

![Loan status in chat](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:30?width=995&height=268)

Review terms before confirming. Eligibility, interest and repayment timing are controlled by server and bank policy.

## Credit

`/account credit` shows your credit score. Late or missed payments may affect future offers.
