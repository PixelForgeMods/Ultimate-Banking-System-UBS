# Claim tools

> Select shop, stockroom, bank and safe regions.

## Selection mode

Claim actions in the management screens launch a temporary world selection mode. Choose the two corners or target blocks, inspect the outline and validation result, then apply and save.

For a stockroom region, left-click the first corner, right-click the opposite corner and check the dimensions shown in the Selection panel. Enter applies the pending selection. Add another region only after the previous one is confirmed; Esc opens the exit decision.

![Stockroom claim corners and outlined region](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:45?width=2560&height=1440)

![Successful stockroom region addition](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:46?width=2560&height=1440)

Delivery pallets are selected as individual staged targets. Overlapping another owner's shop or bank claim is rejected even when their outline is hidden.

## Visibility and safety

The claim HUD replaces ordinary controls while selecting. Use its on-screen save/discard actions so you do not leave a partial setup.

![Save and Exit claim-mode confirmation](screenshot:ultimatebankingsystem:47?width=2560&height=1440)

ClaimOutlinesShowAllPlayers controls whether others' claim outlines are visible; it does not weaken ownership checks.
