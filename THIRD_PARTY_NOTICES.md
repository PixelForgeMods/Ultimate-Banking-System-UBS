## ATM receipt printer recording

User-supplied source: `freesound_community-receipt-printing-82897 (1).mp3`.
The filename credits freesound_community; the original author, source URL and
license document were not supplied, and no license is asserted here.

`atm_receipt_print.ogg` combines source 2.70..4.20 seconds (printing pulses) and
8.70..9.20 seconds (finishing buzz). `atm_receipt_tear.ogg` uses 9.84..10.20 seconds
(paper tear). Converted to mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis, filtered at 180 Hz/7 kHz with
8 ms fades and per-section gain adjustment. The original MP3 is unchanged.
